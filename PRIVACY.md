# Bloom — Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 5 November 2026_

Bloom is a habit tracker and to-do list. This policy explains what happens to
your information. The short version:

> **Bloom keeps everything on your device. It does not collect, transmit, or
> sell your data.**

## The Android app

- Habits, tasks, settings, and backups are stored only on your device, in the
  app's private storage.
- The app has no account, no sign-in, and no server. It does not send your data
  anywhere.
- Reminders and the home-screen widget are scheduled and rendered on-device.
- Automatic backups are local restore points inside the app. They are removed
  when you uninstall the app or clear its data, so keep your own copies if your
  data matters to you.
- Deleting a habit or task — or using **Settings → Data & backups → Clear all
  habits and tasks** — removes the data from your device.

### Permissions the app uses

- **Notifications** — only to show the reminders you turn on.
- **Boot / vibrate** — used by the notification system so your reminders survive
  a device restart.

The app requests no other sensitive permissions and contains no third-party
analytics, advertising, or crash-reporting SDKs.

## The web version

If you run or use the web version, habits and tasks are stored by the server it
connects to (your own Bloom backend). Whoever operates that server is
responsible for that data and its security. The app does not send anything to
the project's authors.

## Children

Bloom is a general-purpose productivity app and is not directed at children. It
collects no personal data from anyone.

## Changes

This policy may be updated over time. The current version always lives in this
repository.
